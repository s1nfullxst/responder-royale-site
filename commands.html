Commands

Everything the bot can do, organised by who can use it.

Play the game

Available to every player.

/firetruck picture

View a random emergency vehicle picture.

/firetruck daily

Claim a daily points or vehicle reward.

/firetruck collection [player]

View a collection.

/firetruck battleleaderboard

Show the battle leaderboard.

/firetruck profile [player]

View a player game profile.

/firetruck leaderboard

Show the main leaderboard.

/firetruck streakleaderboard

See the longest correct-answer streaks.

/firetruck battle

Challenge another player to a vehicle battle.

/firetruck battleaccept

Choose a vehicle and accept a battle.

/firetruck tradeoffer

Offer one of your vehicles in a trade.

/firetruck trades

View open trade offers.

/firetruck accept

Accept an open trade offer.

/firetruck cancel

Cancel one of your trade offers.

Moderation

/moderation warnings

View a member's warnings.

/moderation clearwarnings

Clear all warnings for a member.

/moderation timeout

/moderation kick

Kick a member.

/moderation ban

Ban a member.

/moderation purge

Delete up to 100 recent messages.

/moderation lock

Lock the current channel.

/moderation unlock

Unlock the current channel.
