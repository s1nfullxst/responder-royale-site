Commands

Every available command, grouped by what it does.

Game & collection

Available to players.

/firetruck picture

View a random emergency vehicle.

/firetruck daily

Claim your daily reward.

/firetruck missions

View your three daily missions.

/firetruck missionclaim

Claim completed mission rewards.

/firetruck achievements [player]

View unlocked achievements.

/firetruck profile [player]

View level, points, wins and achievements.

/firetruck collection [player]

View a vehicle collection.

/firetruck leaderboard

Show the points leaderboard.

/firetruck streakleaderboard

Show the longest answer streaks.

/firetruck battleleaderboard

Show the battle leaderboard.

/firetruck battle / battleaccept

Challenge and battle another player.

/firetruck tradeoffer / trades / accept / cancel

Create and manage vehicle trades.

Tickets & support

Private help channels for players and staff.

/ticket open [reason]

Open a private support ticket.

/ticket close [reason]

Close and lock a ticket.

/ticket add

Add a member to a ticket.

/ticket remove

Remove a member from a ticket.

Moderation & automod

Requires the matching Discord staff permission.

/moderation setup

Configure logs, automod, invite blocking and spam protection.

/moderation addword / removeword / words

Manage the server's word filter.

/moderation warn / warnings / clearwarnings

Manage member warnings.

/moderation timeout

Temporarily timeout a member.

/moderation kick / ban

Remove a member from the server.

/moderation purge

Delete up to 100 recent messages.

/moderation lock / unlock

Close or reopen the current channel.

Bot-owner events

Restricted controls for running the game.

/responder spawn / end

Start or end a vehicle round.

/responder doublepoints

Start a double-points event.

/responder rapidspawn

Temporarily spawn vehicles more frequently.

/responder eventstop

Stop all active events.

/responder announce

Send an announcement through the bot.

/responder points

Add or remove player points.

/responder give / remove / collection

Manage and inspect player collections.

/responder backup

Post a complete collection backup.
