WHO WE ARE

The team behind Responder Royale.

We are a small founder-led team building a public Discord game for emergency-vehicle fans. Together we develop the bot and website, run the community, test releases, and keep the service reliable.

OUR MISSION

Make discovering, collecting and learning about emergency vehicles fun for Discord communities around the world.
