Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Information we store

Responder Royale may store Discord user IDs, display names, collections, scores, achievements, streaks, battle and trade information, warnings, moderation reasons, and server configuration needed to provide its features.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the game, moderate servers where the bot is configured to do so, provide support, and improve the bot. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

What we do not collect

We do not collect passwords, payment details, or private messages outside of channels where the bot is intentionally used.

Storage and sharing

Information is processed through Discord and the services used to host the bot. Data is not shared with unrelated third parties except where required to operate these services or comply with law.

Deletion requests

You may request deletion of your game data by contacting the Responder Royale support server and including your Discord user ID.

Changes

We may update this policy as the bot changes. The current version is always available on this page.
