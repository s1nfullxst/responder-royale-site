Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Information we store

Responder Royale may store Discord user IDs, display names, collections, scores, achievements, streaks, battle and trade information, warnings, moderation reasons, and server configuration needed to provide its features. It may also process messages sent in channels where the bot is enabled when needed to recognise game guesses, respond to commands, or provide configured moderation features.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the game, moderate servers where the bot is configured to do so, provide support, and improve the bot. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

What we do not collect

We do not collect passwords, payment details, or private messages outside of channels where the bot is intentionally used.

Storage and sharing

Information is processed through Discord, Wispbyte for bot hosting, and Supabase for public website statistics. Wispbyte’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy apply to its hosting services. We do not sell personal data or share it with unrelated third parties, except where needed to operate these services or where required by law.

Deletion requests

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your game data by contacting the Responder Royale support server and including your Discord user ID. We may keep limited data only where required by law or necessary to resolve abuse, security, or account issues.

Changes

We may update this policy as the bot changes. The current version is always available on this page.

Retention and security

We keep data only for as long as it is needed to run Responder Royale, provide support, protect the service, or meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to help protect data from unauthorised access.

How to contact us

For privacy questions, data requests, or reports, contact us through the Responder Royale support server.
